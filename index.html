Everything About Cherries

Discover the beauty, taste, and benefits of cherries.

1. Sweet cherries are delicious fruits with a juicy texture and a naturally sweet flavor.

2. Each cherry has soft flesh surrounding a single hard pit at its center.

3. Cherry trees produce beautiful blossoms in spring and delicious fruits in the warmer months.

4. Cherries are used in pies, cakes, smoothies, jams, and many other sweet treats.
